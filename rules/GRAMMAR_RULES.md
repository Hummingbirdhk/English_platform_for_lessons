# Grammar Rules

Use current standard English. Never invent rules or simplify them until they become false.

For Grade 6 A2 learners, explain the useful core rule before exceptions. Avoid unnecessary linguistic terminology.

Important grammar rules should normally include a concise Russian explanation and a simple English version. Examples should include natural Russian translation where it helps understanding.

A grammar block should normally cover meaning/use, form, examples, relevant negatives/questions, one or two common mistakes, and short practice.
