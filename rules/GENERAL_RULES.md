# General Teaching Rules

The platform is for clear, visual, interactive English lessons for Grade 6 learners.

Default level: A2. Use early B1 material only when it serves the student and remains understandable.

Prefer clear, useful, contextual, interactive teaching over long theory.

Student-facing content must be natural, age-appropriate, concise, and free from AI filler, corporate language, placeholder text, broken encoding, random symbols, or developer notes.

Educational correctness is more important than generating content quickly.
