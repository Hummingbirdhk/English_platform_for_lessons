# Vocabulary Rules

Vocabulary must be current, useful, natural, age-appropriate, and connected to the lesson topic.

Prefer useful phrases and collocations over isolated words, for example `have breakfast`, `go to school`, and `do homework`.

Keep new vocabulary manageable. When useful, include translation, example sentence, translation of the example, pronunciation, and common collocations.

Do not increase difficulty with obscure vocabulary for its own sake.
